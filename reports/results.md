## Top SHAP drivers

- **bank**: Age, NumOfProducts, IsActiveMember, Balance, Gender
- **ecommerce**: Tenure, Complain, NumberOfAddress, PreferedOrderCat, CashbackAmount
- **retail**: recency_days, frequency, purchases_prev_90, purchases_last_90, tenure_days
- **subscription**: AccountAge, AverageViewingDuration, ContentDownloadsPerMonth, ViewingHoursPerWeek, MonthlyCharges
